# ☄️ Comets of Web3

**Biggest Central & Eastern Europe Blockchain Builders Community**

We are a community-driven organization building the blockchain ecosystem across Central Eastern Europe, connecting developers, designers, founders, and researchers through education, events, and real-world opportunities.

🌐 [cometsweb3.space](https://cometsweb3.space) · 📬 contact@cometsweb3.space

---

## 🚀 What We Do

- **Community** - 10,000+ builders connected across CEE is our goal
- **Education** - Developer cohorts, workshops, hackathons, and technical support
- **Opportunities** - Hiring pipelines, grants, collaborations, and funding
- **Ecosystem Growth** - Partnering with top protocols to onboard and educate builders
